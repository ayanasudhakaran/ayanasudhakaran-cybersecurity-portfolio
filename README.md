## Professional Statement
I am a cybersecurity professional in training, transitioning into the field of cybersecurity with a background that combines forensic science, forensic accounting, and customer-facing operations. My academic and professional experiences have strengthened my investigative mindset, analytical thinking, attention to detail, problem-solving abilities, and commitment to accuracy and confidentiality.

My experience in forensic science introduced me to structured investigation and evidence-based analysis, while my work in hospitality strengthened my ability to communicate clearly, handle problems under pressure, follow procedures, and work with sensitive information. Together, these experiences have shaped the way I approach problems: I like to understand what happened, examine the available evidence, identify the underlying cause, and determine how the issue can be addressed.

I am particularly interested in understanding how security vulnerabilities occur, how they can be identified, and how organizations can reduce their security risks. My curiosity about technology and enjoyment of solving problems and puzzles have motivated me to develop my cybersecurity knowledge through hands-on learning and practical projects.

As I continue building my career in cybersecurity, my goal is to combine my investigative mindset, attention to detail, communication skills, and willingness to learn to help organizations protect their systems and information, identify security risks, and respond effectively to security challenges.
